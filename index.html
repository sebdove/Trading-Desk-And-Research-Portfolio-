The project

A trading desk built on evidence

PVSRA Vector Desk is a desktop app that runs four trading bots side by side: a crypto bot, a Bitcoin trend bot, a stock bot and a futures momentum bot. Every bot can trade on paper inside the app. The crypto, trend and stock bots can also trade real money through Binance or Alpaca once live trading is deliberately switched on.

It began as a browser chart for PVSRA vector candles and grew, one tested release at a time, into a desk of four bots sharing one pot of money. The goal was easy to say and hard to reach. Keep the returns of the best bot, and cut the worst fall in account value from around 47% to near 20%. Each change had to prove itself on years of market data before it went in, and many ideas did not survive that test.

  • One screen for every bot. Account value, open positions, trades and the reason behind each signal.
  • Paper or live. Paper trades run inside the app. Live trading needs the word LIVE typed in and a second confirmation.
  • A head start from 2020. A new paper account replays the bot's own rules from 1 January 2020 on saved market data, so every reset gives the same result.
  • The desk portfolio. One pot of money shared by the bots and re-split every quarter, calmer bots getting more.
  • Bubble Watch. A monthly S&P 500 bubble reading shown beside the stock bot, for information only.

How it works

Four steps, every day

Every bot runs the same loop. The example underneath follows one crypto trade through it.

  1. 1WatchEach bot downloads the latest prices and trading volumes from Binance or Alpaca.
  2. 2DecideIts rules turn those numbers into a decision: buy, sell or wait.
  3. 3TradeOn paper the app records the trade with real costs. Live, the order goes to the exchange.
  4. 4ShareEvery three months the desk moves money towards the calmer bots.

For example

  1. WatchA coin closes above its 50-day average with heavy volume.
  2. DecideThe crypto bot's signals agree and Bitcoin is in an uptrend, so it buys.
  3. TradeIt sets a stop below the price. Once the trade is well up, the stop follows the price to lock in gains.
  4. ShareAt the start of the next quarter, if the crypto bot has been swinging hard, the desk gives it a smaller share.

75 automated tests run before every new version. They check that a bot run day by day ends exactly where its replay ends, that money moved by the desk arrives once, and that the app's results match the research.

The research

How 28 tests built the desk

Five stages, each ending in new releases. Every study asked one question and compared the options on real market data. The badge shows what happened next: shipped into the app, kept as the base for the next test, or dropped. Open a study for its full comparison: the ★ gold row in each table is the winner or the option that went forward. "R" is the amount risked on one trade; "typical" is the middle of 20 test runs.

The question

Train the burst rule on other bubbles (countries, bubble stocks, crypto, commodities), test it blind, then once more on a vault of 90 markets never looked at.

The rule that held up

Out one monthly volatility below the 200-day average

The band scales to each market: about 4% for the S&P 500, 10–17% for Bitcoin. After a deep fall it gets back in on a close above the 50-day average. In the vault it gave up about 0.2% a year against buy and hold but cut the worst fall by about 16 points. Fast crashes (GameStop 2021, China 2015) still get missed.

Download it and run it on paper

The desk is a Windows app. It installs in a minute and starts every bot on paper, so nothing trades real money unless you switch it on yourself.

  • Crypto, trend and momentum bots work on paper straight away using Binance's public market data.

The work

What went into it

Research

  • Back-tests of every change on 2020 to 2026
  • Settings chosen on older years, judged on unseen ones
  • Typical results from 20 runs, not the best run
  • Checks for today's-list bias in coins and S&P 500 stocks
  • A worst-drop target, met by sharing money across bots

Engineering

  • Electron desktop app with a Windows installer
  • Each bot in its own process
  • A paper broker that matches the back-test to the cent
  • Saved market history, so resets replay the same data
  • 75 automated tests and app screen checks before each build

Safety

  • API keys encrypted on the computer, never in config files
  • Live trading needs LIVE typed in and a confirmation
  • New models run on paper only until they earn a record
  • The dashboard is read-only and served to this computer only
  • Binance requests spaced out after an IP ban

Where it stands

All four bots and the desk run on paper from a since-2020 head start. These results are back-tests. They use today's lists of coins and S&P 500 stocks, so the past looks a little better than it was, and markets after 2026 will not repeat 2020 to 2026. Nothing here is financial advice.
